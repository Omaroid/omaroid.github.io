Where I bring experience

Designing and improving data platforms with Spark, PySpark and Databricks; data modeling, transformation pipelines, governance, access design and operational constraints.

  • Cloud and lakehouse: Azure data services, Databricks, Spark, Delta-style lakehouse patterns, platform governance and CI/CD.

Why Forward Deployed Engineering

My strongest fit is at the intersection of a customer's real workflow and the data systems behind it. FDE extends familiar platform engineering habits — deep diagnosis, systems integration and production ownership — with rapid product iteration, user discovery and measurable value delivery.

I am working toward FDE / AI Engineering roles; this site does not claim I have already held that exact title.

AI-assisted engineering, with judgment

AI accelerates prototyping and implementation, but it does not remove the need for clear requirements, security boundaries, repeatable tests or evidence-based evaluation.

Problem framing→AI-aided build→Test + review→Deploy + observe

In case studies, I distinguish documented implementation from future evaluation plans. Where code is private, technical detail is shared at a safe level without disclosing source or customer data.
